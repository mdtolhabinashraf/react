# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
experience for everyone participating in this project.

We welcome contributions regardless of experience, background, identity,
nationality, disability, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to feedback.
- Focus criticism on ideas and implementation, not people.
- Respect different backgrounds, experiences, and viewpoints.
- Accept responsibility and correct mistakes when they occur.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, intimidation, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption, and sharing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove unacceptable contributions and may restrict participation when necessary. Reports should be directed privately to the maintainers through the contact channel listed in the repository.

This code of conduct applies to project spaces and public interactions where someone is representing the project.
